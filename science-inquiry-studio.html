Science Inquiry Studio Platform

Science Inquiry Studio supports active science learning with AI-powered activities. Students investigate phenomena, test ideas with simulations, create artifacts, and explain their thinking with evidence while teachers guide the classroom experience.

  • Ready-to-use science activity library
  • Teacher-guided AI for classroom inquiry
  • Science learning and AI literacy in one environment
  • Support for games, simulations, 2D and 3D models, visuals, and charts

Use the official platform to browse activities, create classes, assign work, and launch student experiences.
